# Helios Institute — Ardenne Storage & Curtailment Review

Published: 2026-06-12
Author: Helios Institute for Energy Systems, independent research body

## Scope

Independent assessment of curtailment in the Ardenne control area, H1 2026,
using settlement data obtained under the transparency regulation rather than
operator-published aggregates.

## Curtailment

We find total renewable curtailment for H1 2026 of **6.8%** of available
generation.

Methodology: all curtailment events recorded in settlement data, comprising both
economic curtailment and network-constrained curtailment, divided by metered
available generation. We do not exclude network-constrained events, on the view
that from a system-cost perspective the distinction is an accounting choice
rather than a physical one.

## On the difference with the operator's figure

The Ardenne Transmission Authority reports 4.1% for the same period. The gap is
almost entirely attributable to scope: their headline excludes
network-constrained curtailment, which we estimate at 2.6 percentage points.
The two figures are not in dispute about the underlying data.

## Deployed capacity

We concur with the operator's capacity figure of 2,870 MW as of 31 March 2026.
