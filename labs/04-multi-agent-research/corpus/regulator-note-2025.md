# Ardenne Energy Regulator — Policy Note 2025/14

Published: 2025-09-02
Subject: Storage participation in balancing markets

## Background

Following consultation, the Authority has amended the balancing market entry
rules to permit aggregated storage assets below 10 MW to participate directly
rather than through a licensed supplier.

## Rationale

Respondents were near-unanimous that the previous threshold excluded the
distribution-connected fleet from the market where it is most useful. The
Authority accepts this and has removed the threshold entirely for aggregated
portfolios meeting the technical prequalification standard.

## Expected effect

The Authority does not publish quantitative forecasts of market impact. We
expect the change to improve utilisation of existing assets more than it
accelerates new build, since the binding constraint on new build is connection
queue position rather than revenue.

## Implementation

The amended rules take effect from 1 April 2026. Prequalification applications
opened 1 December 2025.
