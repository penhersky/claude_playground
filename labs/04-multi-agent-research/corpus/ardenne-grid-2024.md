# Ardenne Grid Operator — Annual Storage Report 2024

Published: 2024-11-18
Prepared by: Ardenne Transmission Authority, Network Planning Division

## Deployed capacity

As of 30 September 2024, grid-connected battery storage in the Ardenne control
area stood at **1,240 MW** across 34 sites. This is a 22% increase on the
2023 figure of 1,016 MW.

## Curtailment

Renewable curtailment for the first half of 2024 was **7.9%** of available
generation. Methodology: total curtailed energy (economic and network-constrained
combined) divided by total available renewable generation, measured at the
transmission interface.

## Duration

The fleet-weighted average discharge duration was **1.9 hours**. Sites
commissioned after 2022 average 2.4 hours, reflecting a shift toward longer-duration
configurations for evening peak coverage.

## Note on comparability

Figures in this report are not directly comparable with our 2022 and earlier
publications, which measured capacity at the point of connection rather than at
the transmission interface.
