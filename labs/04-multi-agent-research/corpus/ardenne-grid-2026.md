# Ardenne Grid Operator — Annual Storage Report 2026

Published: 2026-05-30
Prepared by: Ardenne Transmission Authority, Network Planning Division

## Deployed capacity

As of 31 March 2026, grid-connected battery storage in the Ardenne control area
stood at **2,870 MW** across 61 sites. Growth has accelerated: the compound
annual rate since 2024 is approximately 52%.

## Curtailment

Renewable curtailment for the first half of 2026 was **4.1%** of available
generation. Methodology: **economic curtailment only**, divided by total
available renewable generation, measured at the transmission interface.
Network-constrained curtailment is reported separately in Annex C and is
excluded from this headline figure.

## Duration

Fleet-weighted average discharge duration is now **3.1 hours**.

## Methodology change

From 2025 onward, the headline curtailment figure covers economic curtailment
only. Readers comparing with 2024 and earlier should note that those figures
combined economic and network-constrained curtailment and are therefore higher
by construction.
